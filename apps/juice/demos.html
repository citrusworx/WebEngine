Tabs

A [tabs] root, [tabs-list] strip, [tab] triggers, and [tab-panel] panels. Selection dual-writes [active] and aria-selected. Hidden panels use native hidden.

Modal

Openers use aria-controls pointing at [modal-overlay]. Close with the overlay close control, Escape, or the backdrop. modal-overlay="static" keeps the backdrop from dismissing.

Drawer

Edge is [drawer] / [drawer="left"] / [drawer="right"]. Size is a separate [drawer-size="sm|lg"] on the panel — not on the edge attribute. Openers still use aria-controls.

Toast

Authors place [toast-region] and [toast] nodes. There is no aria-controls opener — show and dismiss go through createToast(). Duration is toast-duration in milliseconds; "0" / "Infinity" is sticky. Not a dialog.

Your changes were written. Auto-dismisses in 3s.

Bottom-left region. toast-duration="0" stays until dismiss, close, or Escape.

Popover

Use [popover-root] and [popover-panel]. Never a bare popover attribute — that is the native HTML Popover API. Openers use aria-controls. Placement is popover-root="bottom|top|left|right".

Tooltip On master

Triggers prefer aria-describedby pointing at [tooltip-root]. The surface is [tooltip-panel] — never a bare tooltip attribute, and not native title. Hover or focus the controls. No focus trap.
