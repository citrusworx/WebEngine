Add composable `blur="sm"` and `blur="md"` utilities (`6px` / `16px` backdrop-filter). Standalone use needs no `surfaceTone`. Combined with a tone, explicit `blur` overrides frost length only so tone background, border, and shadow stay. Optional `--juice-blur-sm` / `--juice-blur-md` restyles; themes do not bind a second blur scale. This finishes the A–C surface utility pass.
